Dashboard Monitoring Anomali Pelanggan Listrik

Period / Company : PT PLN (Persero) UP3 SURABAYA SELATAN Role : Data Analyst Internship Tools : PowerBI

To monitor and classify the electricity usage status of all customers across five areas in South Surabaya (ULP Ngagel, ULP Rungkut, ULP Darmo Permai, ULP Gedangan, and ULP Dukuh Kupang) into three categories—Normal, Suspicious, and Critical—based on an automated behavior score.

Objective

To replace manual electricity usage anomaly detection processes with an automated, visual system, thereby accelerating the identification of customers with potential anomalies for follow-up action by field personnel.

Hotel Management in Netherlands

Period : Course Project Tools : Tableau

Primary Function

This dashboard serves as a hub for hospitality management analysis, providing a comprehensive data summary that includes the total number of hotels (512 properties), total and average pricing, aggregate ratings, review counts, and detailed information on room types across various regions in the Netherlands. The system features interactive data visualizations, such as KPI summary cards, ranked lists of hotels by name and region, bar charts for comparing prices and ratings across states or cities, donut charts illustrating the distribution of reviews and room types, and an interactive geospatial map to visualize the spatial distribution of the hotels.

System Strengths

The system’s strength lies in its ability to present multi-perspective information through seamlessly integrated "Summary" and "Customer Intent" navigation menus. It includes dynamic data filtering and location-based search capabilities (covering cities such as Amsterdam, Eindhoven, Rotterdam, and Utrecht), as well as cross-filtering functionality between tables, charts, and maps—enabling users to explore spatial data and hotel attributes in a responsive and in-depth manner.

Objective

The development of this dashboard aims to assist stakeholders, tourism industry observers, and hotel managers in analyzing hospitality market trends in the Netherlands, understanding customer preferences and intent, and comparing prices and ratings across different regions or room types. By presenting large datasets through concise, interactive visualizations, the system is designed to support strategic decision-making in hospitality business management and facilitate accurate regional mapping.

Climate Change and Agriculture Analysis

Period : Course Project Tools : PowerBI

Primary Function

This dashboard serves as an interactive analytical hub for monitoring the relationship between climate change and the global agricultural sector from 1990 to 2024. The system incorporates various key data visualization components, including filters for time ranges and country selection, bar charts showing total precipitation by region, a map illustrating the global geographic distribution of extreme weather events, line graphs tracking average temperature trends over time, a table detailing regions affected by extreme weather, and a dedicated radar chart section for carbon dioxide emissions.

System Advantages

The system’s strength lies in its ability to provide an integrated multivariate analysis, combining agricultural, meteorological, and geospatial data into a single, intuitive interface. Equipped with interactive filters based on year and region—such as a country search function and a list of extreme weather-affected areas—the dashboard enables users to conduct in-depth, flexible data exploration and quickly interpret temperature and precipitation trends across different regions.

Objective

The development of this dashboard aims to assist researchers, policymakers, and environmental observers in analyzing the impact of climate change on the agricultural sector worldwide. By visually presenting historical data on temperature, precipitation, and extreme weather, the system seeks to support the identification of environmental patterns, facilitate the evaluation of global climate risks, and provide a foundation for formulating sustainable agricultural mitigation strategies.

Dashboard Rain Australia

Period : Course Project Tools : Data Studio

Primary Function

This dashboard serves as a hub for monitoring and exploring historical weather and rainfall data in Australia, covering the period from January 1, 2015, to June 25, 2017. The system features interactive date-range filtering and location selection, allowing users to monitor daily meteorological parameters—including specific readings taken at 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM—alongside a geospatial map for visualizing regional distribution.

System Advantages

The system’s key strengths lie in its integrated multi-page navigation (covering daily summaries, 9:00 AM data, and 3:00 PM data) and a diverse range of data visualizations, such as line graphs, pie charts, bar charts, and distribution maps. Furthermore, the dashboard offers a high level of interactivity, with search functions and location checkboxes that make exploring large-scale datasets intuitive.

Objective

The purpose of this dashboard is to facilitate the analysis of climate patterns, temperature fluctuations, and rainfall trends across various Australian cities, thereby supporting weather evaluation and forecasting. Additionally, the system aims to transform tens of thousands of rows of raw meteorological data into concise, easily understandable visual information, ultimately accelerating the decision-making process.
